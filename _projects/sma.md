<section class="project-story">
  <p class="project-meta">Media art and product design / mechanical designer and developer</p>

  <p class="project-lede">
    Inspired by the subtle, silent blooming of a sunflower, Quietly Blooming is a shape-changing lamp that transforms both its light and its form in response to the user's "Qi": a self-defined rhythm reflecting sleep pattern and mood.
  </p>

  <div class="project-media project-media--wide">
    {% include figure.liquid loading="lazy" path="assets/img/projects/sma/WechatIMG155 2.jpg" alt="Quietly Blooming shape-changing lamp" class="project-image" %}
  </div>

  <p>
    We moved away from joint-and-gear-driven movement because it often feels rigid. Drawing from shape-memory research, the lamp uses nitinol and folded paper to create petal-like motion through heat rather than mechanical actuation.
  </p>

  <div class="project-gallery project-gallery--two">

    </div>
  </div>

  <p>
    As mechanical engineer and developer, I designed the movement structure and coordinated it with the LED lighting. I wrote the Arduino control logic and designed a custom PCB to assemble multiple SMA actuators through centralized heat control.
  </p>

  <p>
    Using origami-folded paper combined with nitinol, I programmed the flower's different shape states through pre-heating. The project was exhibited at Huawei and awarded the A' Design Award in Italy.
  </p>
