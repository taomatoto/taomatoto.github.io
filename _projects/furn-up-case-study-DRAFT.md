<section class="project-story">
  <p class="project-meta">Service design / circular furniture / Barcelona Design Week 2022</p>

  <p class="project-lede">
    Furn.up is a concept for making second-hand furniture feel desirable, social, and worth keeping. The project reframes used furniture as a story-carrying object that can be repaired, customized, and passed on.
  </p>

  <div class="project-media project-media--wide project-media--contain">
    {% include figure.liquid loading="lazy" path="assets/img/projects/furnup/1.png" alt="Furn.up project overview" class="project-image project-image--contain" %}
  </div>

  <p>
    The platform connects peer-to-peer furniture exchange with local upcycling workshops. A chair, table, or lamp can move from one home to another with its previous story, condition notes, and possible transformations attached.
  </p>

  <div class="project-gallery project-gallery--two">
    <div class="project-media project-media--contain">
      {% include figure.liquid loading="lazy" path="assets/img/projects/furnup/2.png" alt="Furn.up interface screen" class="project-image project-image--contain" %}
    </div>
    <div class="project-media project-media--contain">
      {% include figure.liquid loading="lazy" path="assets/img/projects/furnup/3.png" alt="Furn.up interface screen" class="project-image project-image--contain" %}
    </div>
  </div>

  <p>
    I designed the service journey, visual system, and platform experience. The work explores circular design as a social behavior: people do not only buy an object, they learn how to repair it, personalize it, and keep its story moving.
  </p>
